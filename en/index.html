Serbian payment slip generator with IPS QR code

Fill the form and download an A4 PDF holding three payment slips at their true size, 210 × 99 mm, each carrying an NBS IPS QR code. It prints on plain paper: the blank itself is drawn, so no pre-printed stock is needed.

  • QR code to the National Bank of Serbia's specification — pay by scanning
  • Blank geometry measured from real slips and checked against a reference
  • Everything runs in the browser: no payment details leave the device
  • Interface in English, Serbian and Russian
